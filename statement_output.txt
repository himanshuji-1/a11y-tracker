Animepahe.ch is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying relevant accessibility standards. Our goal is to provide an inclusive online environment where all users can access information and functionality with ease, regardless of their abilities or the assistive technologies they use. We are actively working to increase the accessibility and usability of our website and, in doing so, adhere to many of the available standards and guidelines. Our ongoing efforts aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. This includes regular automated scans and manual reviews to identify and address potential barriers. We acknowledge that while significant progress has been made, our most recent automated scan on July 11, 2026, identified 12 serious issues that are currently under active remediation. We are dedicated to resolving these and any further identified issues promptly to achieve our compliance target. If you encounter any accessibility barriers while using Animepahe.ch, please do not hesitate to contact us at accessibility@yourcompany.com. Your feedback is invaluable as it helps us identify areas for improvement and continually enhance our accessibility efforts. We appreciate your patience and understanding as we work towards making our platform fully accessible to all.
